# Dependencies and original notices

- ROS 2, Nav2, SLAM Toolbox, OctoMap, RViz and CycloneDDS: external dependencies installed through their distributions, under their own licenses.
- Arducam depth SDK: external; header and library required by the driver, not bundled.
- Adafruit BNO055 and Unified Sensor: external Arduino dependencies; retain their upstream notices.
- `microM`: DAGU Micro Magician library attributed to Russell Cameron in the recovered archive. Its sources are not included because the available copy has no explicit license statement.
- Recovered ROS packages retain their original MIT or Apache-2.0 declarations. The root GPL license does not replace third-party notices.

This bundle includes only source and mesh resources selected from the robot project. No proprietary SDK binaries, credentials or diagnostic recordings are included.
